FAQ & Policies
Donation pickups You choose what to donate and where — we deliver your load to Deseret Industries or Goodwill as-is. If the donation center declines an item, we'll dispose of it for you at our standard rate.
What happens after I submit? We review your photo and email you a flat-rate price, usually within a few hours. You'll get a Stripe payment link to pay a 50% deposit and lock in your spot. No commitment until you pay.
Do you haul hazardous materials? We can't haul paint, chemicals, or other hazardous waste. Logan City Landfill offers a free Household Hazardous Waste drop-off — please remove those items before pickup and we'll haul everything else.
